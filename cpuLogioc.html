CPU and Logic Understanding

A hands-on digital logic and computer architecture project

Project Overview

This page is dedicated to documenting my journey of connecting digital logic gates and circuits to learn how they can be combined to form larger computer components.

My long-term goal is to build and understand a simple CPU using individual logic chips, breadboards, and supporting electronic components.

PCB/Analog/logic designs

I am currently learning about logic-gate pinouts, breadboard power, NAND gates, Boolean logic, safe circuit testing, and the role of integrated circuits in a CPU.

Planned Components

Future stages may include basic logic gates, adders, registers, counters, memory, a clock circuit, an arithmetic logic unit, and control logic.

Current Progress

The project is currently in its early learning and experimentation stage. This page will be updated with circuit diagrams, photographs, test results, challenges, and completed milestones.
